## Neutral initials avatars

Abdullah Al-Juhany, Abdul Muhsin Al-Qasim, Abdurrahman As-Sudais, Ibrahim Al-Dossari, and Mishary Rashid Alafasy currently use initials, not photographs. Their audio remains available.

Research on 4 November 2026 found candidates for Al-Juhany and As-Sudais on Wikimedia Commons; full provenance review and integration are still pending. The Mishary candidate `File:Мишари Рашид.jpg` relies on a broad free-use claim at quranic.ru which has not been independently confirmed. The Al-Qasim result was a group photograph without sufficient identification of each person for a portrait crop. No suitable Ibrahim Al-Dossari portrait was verified in this pass. These findings do not assert that licensed photographs cannot be found.

The six audio IDs and EQuran sources are unchanged. Fourteen additional reciters are not enabled until per-verse audio sources and usage terms are verified. No generated or guessed audio URLs are added.
